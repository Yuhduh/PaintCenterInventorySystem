# System UI enhancement

Mode: Operate

Audience: Grade A Paint Center staff working repeatedly across counter and back-office tasks.

Job: Move from orientation to action quickly, scan dense operational state, and complete product, inventory, mixing, sales, reporting, audit, and access workflows without losing context.

Constraints: Preserve all routes, role visibility, validation, Livewire behavior, confirmation dialogs, and factual copy. Responsive web, keyboard access, reduced motion, and high-density tables are first-class requirements.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The interface behaves like a precise modern color studio: a stable ink-and-paper workspace organized by measured swatches, not a collection of generic white cards. It refuses the bland admin-template grid while keeping operational familiarity.

OWN-WORLD: Deep blue-ink navigation, clean mineral-white work surfaces, cyan as the single action color, and small controlled warm/cool swatches for status. Corners are confident rather than pill-heavy; depth is soft and directional; tables, filters, forms, and modals share one exact component grammar.

STORY: Staff arrive, immediately understand where they are, see the state that needs attention, perform one primary task, and receive clear feedback. Dense modules read as composed workbenches with strong page titles, grouped controls, legible totals, and calm exception states.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The authenticated shell anchors a dark studio rail at left and a quiet sticky utility bar above a generous work canvas. The login opens with a kinetic stack of labeled color-workflow bands beside a focused sign-in card; on mobile the identity compresses above the form without decorative overflow.

FORM: Established-world expansion in Operate mode; the incumbent cyan/ink system is the visual authority, so no replacement-direction roll is required. Seed key: incumbent-cyan-operations-v1. Signature interaction: one orchestrated paint-swatch sequence on login, with all other motion limited to purposeful state transitions.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
